\section{Methods and verification}
\label{sec:methods-verification}

\paragraph{AI-assisted research tools.}
OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6, accessed September 2026) was used to assist literature discovery and source navigation, organization of analytical work, algebraic and code cross-checking, and preparation and review of reproducibility scripts. AI-generated output was not treated as evidence or as a substitute for proof. Literature claims and citations were checked against source materials, while mathematical claims were checked against the frozen theory record, symbolic verification, regression and counterexample tests, and Lean formalization where applicable. The AI assistance did not determine the research question, model, key arguments, or conclusions. All AI-assisted output used in the research process was reviewed by the author, who retains full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the final work. No AI-generated or AI-edited visual content appears in the manuscript.
