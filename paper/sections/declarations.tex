\section*{Declarations}

\paragraph{Data availability.}
Data sharing is not applicable to this article because no datasets were generated or analysed during the study.

\paragraph{Code and reproducibility materials.}
Code supporting the symbolic verification, regression and counterexample tests, deterministic exposition outputs, and the documented Lean formalization is provided as anonymous supporting information for peer review. A public version of the reproducibility materials will be cited upon acceptance.

\paragraph{AI-assisted research tools.}
OpenAI ChatGPT was used for literature discovery and algebraic and code cross-checking. AI-generated outputs were not treated as evidence: cited sources were checked against source materials, and mathematical claims were checked against the frozen theory record, symbolic verification, regression tests, and Lean formalization where applicable. The AI assistance did not determine the paper's key arguments or conclusions, and the author retains full responsibility for the final content.
