## 2. Bounded mathematics RED TEAM: 'simply add more regions/networks' fails as an independent new theorem

Let a finite undirected graph with nonnegative symmetric weights `w_ij`, weighted degree `d_i=sum_j w_ij`; each government has `x_i∈[0,1]`, U direct premium `Δ>0`, upstream welfare fraction `α∈(0,1)`, cross-region A>0. The smallest literal extension of the frozen 2-region model is

```math
W_i(x)=b_D+Δx_i+A∑_{j~i}w_ij{αx_i(1−x_j)+(1−α)(1−x_i)x_j}.
```

**X1, algebraically exact**: `∂W_i/∂x_i=Δ+α A d_i−A∑_j w_ij x_j`. Thus the *exact potential*

```math
Φ(x)=∑_i(Δ+α A d_i)x_i−A∑_{i<j}w_ij x_i x_j
```

has `∂Φ/∂x_i=∂W_i/∂x_i`; as each W_i is affine in own x_i, unilateral payoff differences exactly equal potential differences. Compactness of `[0,1]^n` implies Φ attains a maximum: pure-strategy NE exists by standard potential-game theory. NOT an original existence theorem (Monderer–Shapley 1996). Furthermore, since Φ is multi-affine, a corner maximum exists. Interior indifferent NE may also exist; no claim of exclusively binary equilibria.

**X2, exact simple domination condition:** If `Δ>(1−α)A max_i d_i`, U strictly dominates any lower x_i for ALL opponents, so all-U is the unique Nash equilibrium. Social sum is

```math
SW(x)=n b_D + Δ∑_i x_i+A∑_{i<j}w_ij[x_i(1−x_j)+(1−x_i)x_j].
```

Switch one vertex i to D from all-U, holding neighbors at U: `ΔSW=A d_i−Δ`. Therefore if also `Δ<A max_i d_i` (and a positive-weight edge exists), the unique all-U equilibrium is strictly inefficient. The open wedge

```math
(1−α)A d_max < Δ < A d_max
```

is a direct network generalization of the old 2-region wedge **with no new intellectual mechanism**. It is best-response-equivalent on binary profiles to a weighted graph two-color anti-coordination game with node-specific profits: compare own-strategy gain `Δ+α A d_i−A∑ w_ij x_j` to an edge-cut payoff of weight `A w_ij/2` and a U-color premium `Δ+(α−1/2)A d_i`. Any negative color premium can be shifted by a playerwise constant to satisfy nonnegative-profit formulations.

**Falsification verdict for this particular claim:** mathematical identities X1/X2 are useful diagnostics, but pure Nash existence, potential structure, basic network wedge and vertex-profit heterogeneity **DO NOT by themselves pass a JME/JET-level new-theorem gate**. Existence is under established potential-game theory; finite weighted graph/color-profit cases are especially close to Carosi & Monaco 2018. No blanket rejection of ALL network/equilibrium generalizations is warranted: a genuinely new, nonabsorbed theorem under economically meaningful relaxed conditions would be a separate candidate.
