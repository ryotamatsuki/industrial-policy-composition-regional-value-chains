## B. Frozen parent model and exact result
Original repo main THEORY_FREEZE.md and paper/sections read on Oct 10:
W_i(x_i,x_j)=b_D+Delta*x_i+alpha*A*x_i*(1-x_j)+(1-alpha)*A*(1-x_i)*x_j.
Delta>0,A>0,alpha in (0,1); x_i in [0,1] is fraction of fixed regional industrial-policy capacity put into upstream U. The rest is downstream D.
For Delta < A < Delta/(1-alpha), unique Nash (1,1); coordinated same-capacity planner prefers (1,0) or (0,1); welfare gap A-Delta>0. Paper deliberately omits an actionable contract, funding instrument, institutional implementation, legal appropriations, binding commitments or endogenous production decisions.
Hence an ORIGINAL normatively meaningful implementation result may be a justified theorem extension. Adding a repeated normal-form public-good game for the sake of feature count is not.

## C. What the editor response could already say; what remains unexplained
Conditional on binding role assignment, move from (1,1) to (1,0): U-region gain alpha*A; D-region gain (1-alpha)*A-Delta, negative in wedge. Transfer t U->D yields bilateral participation constraints
Delta-(1-alpha)*A <= t <= alpha*A
(strict inequalities yield strict Pareto gains). Nonempty exactly when A>=Delta. A transfer cap B adds trivial feasibility B>=Delta-(1-alpha)*A (for a transfer without taxation or enforcement costs).
**These are economic illustrations, NOT a publishable standalone original theorem.** They have already been worked in revision/post-jrs/H01_VOLUNTARY_CONTRACT_CHALLENGE.md with narrow protocol and a continuous-off-path applicability caution.
