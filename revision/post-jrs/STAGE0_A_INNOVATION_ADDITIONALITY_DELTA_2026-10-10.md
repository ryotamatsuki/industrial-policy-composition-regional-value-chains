## A0 — Exact economic question and unit of comparison

**Research question.** When competing local governments spend a *fixed* innovation-policy budget on (i) geographically appropriable attraction/retention subsidies versus (ii) innovation support whose benefits spill across regions, under what economically justified conditions does competition raise local observed patents or R&D employment while **reducing or increasing total genuinely new national innovation** compared with feasible coordination? Why would this sign differ from the ranking of local headline performance?

Define and NEVER conflate: `local measured patents`; `locally performed R&D`; `nationwide new innovation output`; `inventor/firm relocation`; `aggregate real welfare net of fiscal resources`. A fall in the rest-of-country patent count alone does NOT prove pure displacement, and geographic relocation is NOT always national zero sum when agglomeration, congestion and labor-market costs matter.

Economic audience: public economics (fiscal federalism / industrial policies), innovation economics, regional/urban economics. Potential full article only **IF** a robust, source-unabsorbed endogenous policy-composition equilibrium + genuine sign/boundary theorem survives; otherwise short note or no paper.

## A2 — Delta Card (one binding economic distinction)

**Named parent benchmark:** Camêlo & Sollaci (2025), Prop.2 and §5: subsidy-driven spatial location, nationally relevant innovation growth, and optimal nationwide subsidy allocation; policy vector is assessed as a given/aggregate-planner choice in inspected §5. Confront Ossa (2015), Kondo (2013), Forslid/Okubo (2024), not only one isolated paper.

**UNPROVED change:** local governments face a **fixed budget allocation choice between two genuinely different support instruments**, geographically appropriable (attraction/retention) and non-appropriable (new research production / shared innovation capability), while mobile inventors and firms respond. The task is NOT merely to relabel a generic tax/subsidy as `x_i`. The institutional fiscal accounting, public-good externality and private innovation response must follow defensible economics rather than a tuned payoff fraction.

**UNPROVED theorem hypothesis A-H1:** a *nontrivial, economically interpretable* parameter boundary separates (a) regional support competition that has **positive true nationwide innovation additionality** despite business stealing and (b) local metrics rising while **national innovation additionality is nonpositive**; critically, the sign and welfare ordering can differ from the planner-vs-uniform and generic firm-attraction competition benchmarks. A simple 'own grants rise, own patents rise' or an arbitrarily signed spillover coefficient is **NOT a result**.

**Preferred prospective article unit:** applied theoretical **full paper only conditional on** a new equilibrium/welfare boundary not directly implied by the strongest parents, AND an endogenous regional instrument *composition* rather than one subsidy rate; else choose shorter note or STOP. **Novelty status: UNPROVED; economic significance: high; topic fit: DIRECT; provisional journals: JPET/JRS/RSUE/innovation economics (not selected).**

## A3 — Anti-novelty controls

- Mere 'national new patents vs relocating researchers' is **already in the parent** Camêlo/Sollaci.
- Mere 'decentralized Nash vs national planner' is **already in** Ossa and Kondo; Camêlo discusses competition but does not establish this same new Nash by itself.
- Mere 'innovation subsidies are sometimes welfare-reducing' already in extensive R&D/resource market and network-tax literatures.
- Empirical local gains and adjacent negative effects do not identify whether innovators moved or new ideas were forgone.
- An extra policy instrument without an additional endogenous strategic result does NOT pass.
- A verified nonabsorbed economics-theory theorem can qualify **without** prescribing a specific implementable legal policy.

**Stage 0 A verdict: CONDITIONAL GO FOR ONE SOURCE-FIRST DELTA TEST, no novelty certification**. Exactly one material gate: *whether fixed-budget strategic instrument-composition competition delivers a nontrivial nationally relevant innovation/equilibrium boundary NOT absorbed by Kondo + Forslid/Okubo + Camêlo/Sollaci*.
