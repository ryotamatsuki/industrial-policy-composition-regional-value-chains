## 2. Exact negative control A: a strict 3-regime winner exists WITHOUT new theory

Use Ventura (2019) Prop1 **project-by-project parliamentary voting with N≥3 regions**, with per-project net LOCAL public surplus `β` and **sum** external surplus `ε` to other regions (including financing costs in local if appropriate; not modeling private production). Define `v=β+ε`. For a finite list `P` of project types, compare three **institutional** regimes:

- `W_0=0`: no PUBLICLY FUNDED project (NOT a formal model of no state regulation, firm entry, laissez-faire market GE);
- `W_L=Σ_{p∈P}(β_p+ε_p) 1[β_p≥0]`: regional/parliament project votes;
- `W_N=Σ_{p∈P}(β_p+ε_p) 1[ε_p≥0]`: NATIONAL parliamentary median picks based on aggregate externality sign (N≥3, majority rule and symmetrical allocation of external benefits);
- `W_FB=Σ_{p∈P} max(β_p+ε_p,0)`: omniscient planner benchmark.

By inspection `W_FB≥max(W_0,W_L,W_N)`. For **strict winner per regime** choose:
1. MARKET-STATUS-QUO best: two projects `(β,ε)=(1,−3)` and `(−3,1)`; `(W0,WL,WN)=(0,−2,−2)`.
2. LOCAL best: one project `(4,−1)`; `(W0,WL,WN)=(0,3,0)`.
3. NATIONAL best: one project `(−1,4)`; `(W0,WL,WN)=(0,0,3)`.

These examples are fully arithmetically certified in `revision/post-jrs/code/three_regime_negative_control.py` using Python Fraction. They show **any of three statuses can be welfare-best under Ventura's already published local/national voting criterion when adding the trivial no-project benchmark**. This is a **routine direct corollary**, NOT an original switching theorem or independent new economic result. Note the regional program is not the entire private market. **Do not sell this as discovering when markets should be left alone**; it covers decision to undertake a publicly financed project.

### Structural no-free-lunch argument

If a NATIONAL BENEVOLENT PLANNER has (a) exact same information, (b) access to all regional feasible controls, (c) ability to imitate *every* local-policy strategy profile and (d) no extra implementation/information/political/financing cost, then `max_{s∈S_N} W(s)≥ W(s^L)`, provided `S_N ⊇ {s^L,0}`. Thus national weak dominance of all alternatives is **set inclusion**, not a meaningful empirical or publishable theorem. A three-way switching theorem must endogenously justify a nontrivial national constraint or endogenous response rather than tack on an arbitrary penalty.

## 3. Exact negative control B: splitting POLICY RIGHTS need NOT destroy implementability or optimality

**A stand-alone toy linear ALLOCATION model, NOT Miranda-Pinto GE.** Let two intervention instruments `t=(t1,t2)` influence two welfare-relevant targets `y=(y1,y2)` through `y=B t`, `B=[[1,1],[1,-1]]`, targets `d=(2,0)`. National has both controls; region1 controls `t1`, region2 `t2`. Define `u_i(t)=−(y_i−d_i)^2/2`, aggregate `W=u1+u2`.

- `det B=−2`, rank2: national chooses `t*=(1,1)`, achieving `y=d`, `W=0`.
- Region1 best response: `t1=d1−t2`. Region2 best response: `t2=t1−d2`. The unique pure Nash equilibrium is `t1=t2=1`, also attaining national first best `W=0`.
- Hence the **same COLUMN SET** available separately to local governments has full joint rank; even though effects spill over between target equations, a Nash outcome can implement the planner's target exactly. It is false that **mere allocation of instrument ownership makes the target unimplementable or locally inefficient**.

These are algebraic negative controls, not full IO GE or an originality claim. Any innovation theorem needs constraints on who MAY intervene in WHICH region/industry, region-specific budgets, positive implementability, cross-border tax incidence and actual strategic government objectives. Simply blocking columns in A and comparing linear equation ranks is Miranda-Pinto Lemma1 adapted to arbitrary notation and insufficient originality.
