## V. Stage0 adversarial Delta Cards — NOT proven or externally novel

**Q1 (preferred bounded test, theory with direct regional policy relevance):** Does the **combination of industrial-network subsidy INSTRUMENT FEASIBILITY** (Miranda-Pinto, labor vs intermediates subsidies and rank) with LOCAL-government **instrument jurisdiction/fiscal limitations** and cross-prefecture production spillovers produce a nontrivial sharp condition under which `market/no intervention`, `decentralized policy`, or `national program` is welfare superior? (i) Source theorem: Miranda-Pinto Prop4/5, Besley-Coate Prop2, Lockwood Prop4, Gregor/Šťastná 2012. (ii) One binding change: tools/instrument rights are allocated to distinct local governments, with truly interregional IO link and observed distortions, not only arbitrarily adding fixed administration cost. (iii) Potential different consequence **UNPROVED**: imperfect decentralized control makes some socially useful cross-region subsidy vectors implementable centrally but not in regional noncooperative Nash, while other politically distorted national policies can be welfare-dominated by local budgets or no intervention. (iv) **STOP** if result is a trivial rank restriction, parent bilateral transfer result, or a purely exogenous welfare ranking cost add-on. (v) **Source blocker**: published Gregor2012 theorem and multiple-jurisdiction input allocation literature not exhaustively audited.

**Q2 (alternate general theory):** Endogenous entry may flip a private-market distortion ranking along production network links when sector distortion centrality `ξ_i` changes with intervention; derive a genuine non-marginal welfare sign reversal relative to Liu's `ξ_i-1` first-order measure. (i) Parents Liu 2019 and Mankiw/Whinston 1986, Hausmann/Rodrik 2003, **Poirier 2024** endogenous links, 2026 oligopoly/supplier entry theory. (ii) Hard originality threat: markups/supplier entry endogenous already modeled; 'derivative changes sign' is routine and NOT itself a theorem. (iii) **STOP** absent source-unabsorbed sharp economic structure/threshold.
