## Deduplication and stop constraints

1. **F01 = F02 mathematical content.** These differ by purpose and dissemination, **not** originality; never count two novel papers.
2. F03 source-based program description is not causal inference and is not F05's solved coordination mechanism.
3. F04 causal inference requires externally observed policies and counterfactual comparison; Stage 0 has not measured observable A, alpha or Delta.
4. F05 cannot reuse failed A1 / R01–R10 merely by substituting “public sector” for “private sector”; must show genuinely distinct strategic feedback and policy rule.
5. Editor value gate: if F01 offers nothing beyond a generic strict Nash-planner gap, does not have a real *spatial* dimension or would be an immediate special case of closely published expenditure-composition results, **NO-GO journal route** despite proof validity.
