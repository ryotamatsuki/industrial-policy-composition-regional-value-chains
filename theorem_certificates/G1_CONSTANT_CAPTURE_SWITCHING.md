# G1 — Constant-Capture Switching Lemma

- Classification: sufficient-condition switching theorem, not a general continuous-game theorem.
- Domain: `Delta > 0`, `G > 0`, `0 < lambda < 1`.
- Comparison: a binary policy-configuration switch from a locally attractive activity to a complementary activity.
- Planner switch gain: `G - Delta`.
- Local switch gain: `lambda G - Delta`.
- Thresholds: `G^P = Delta`, `G^N = Delta/lambda`, hence `G^P < G^N`.
- Proof maturity: `PROVED AT CERTIFIED SCOPE`.
- Stage 7.5A: `PASS AFTER SCOPE NARROWING`.
- Prohibited wording: arbitrary continuous portfolios obey the same theorem without additional structure.
