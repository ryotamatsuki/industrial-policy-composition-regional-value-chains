## 3. Theorem-level attainment, not a keyword map
### 3.1 Spatial normative theorem already strong: national instrument sufficiency and price-incidence
P08 source, Eq(2), (3) establishes in a canonical housing/employment/infrastructure urban GE:
dW = Σ_j[Γ^L_j−s^L_j]dL_j + Σ_j[Γ^I_j−s^I_j]dI_j,
where Γ^L_j=γ^L_j(w_j−s^L_j), Γ^I_j=γ^I_j(p_j−s^I_j).
When sufficiently rich instruments available, each margin's optimum corrects its externality; cannot automatically claim the benefit of municipal industrial PARK/subsidies just by an agglomeration externality. P08 Result 5 explicitly excludes investment/housing as an **optimal substitute for labor subsidy in the FIRST BEST**; it does NOT say infrastructure never improves welfare. The authors themselves Eq(16) show residual welfare gradients with labor subsidy constrained. **Unsolved in this parent**: exact best available second-best policy profile under nontrivial pre-existing distortions and locally restricted instruments. **Not unstudied in economics**: P07 QJE optimal spatial sorting, P09 QJE job-market frictions and general second-best tax theory already explore major such departures.

### 3.2 Decentralization may be first-best; administrative ability can reverse centralized-rank intuition
P03 AER (original 2007 WP) efficient spillback condition; P06 original 2014 WP Prop6 low administrative capacity can favor FULL decentralization IF fiscal capacity high enough, while low fiscal capacity leads to partial central bailouts. This formally falsifies blanket "local vs national policy competition is always inefficient" and "stronger local staff implies stronger case for decentralization". P04 2026 also reports a market-integration-dependent LOCAL-vs-CENTRAL welfare preference. **All generic federalism rankings KILLED**.

### 3.3 Subsidy auction/industrial targeting and knowledge already solved
P02 JPE models bidding as private-value English auction, different spatial preferences and firms' rents. P05 SJE symmetry-breaking productive-public-good government competition and input-output upstream industry already. P20 Poirier solves supplier rewiring under central optimal intervention. Thus a local industrial target chosen by "new" bidding + network link selection is a 2-parent composition, not original by itself.

### 3.4 Policy learning and information distortions are distinctly different known models
P10 QJE experiment placement diverges because of strategic learning; P11 NBER government CAREER signals can crowd out private economic discovery. AEA JEL original online appendix P01: policy correlation generated by pure learning need not be true fiscal strategic response, and welfare implications of cooperation differ. This establishes a CRUCIAL identity trap: reactive regional policy mimicking is not evidence of subsidy rivalry or a reason to coordinate. Generic info asymmetry and policy experimentation KILLED.

### 3.5 Public R&D procurement and local administrative effort already linked by published research
P13 Hwang's principal–public buyer–innovator contract and P14 Asseyer's R&D investment with optimal monitoring severely absorb each other's "new" variations; Theilen's delegated monitor produces effort. Pre-existing NO-GO from Stage1 is retained, not resurrected. A municipal staff-capacity constraint is not sufficient novelty.

### 3.6 Region industrial transition has graph-target optimization already
P17 published paper optimizes related/unrelated sector activation orders (time criterion). Simply making "future potential" a state variable in industrial targets repeats its path dependence. Distinct normative criterion with endogenous wages/fiscal/ownership potentially lies outside P17, but it may be standard Markov industry policy and requires adversarial full parent review.

## 4. Three *source-explicit* frontier windows — NOT yet proven truly unexplored
**S1 — second-best, INSTRUMENT-FEASIBLE spatial industry policy:** P08 §5 says first-best theorems rely on no pre-existing distortions, ability to use labor incentives, localized and sector-neutral spillovers; also **explicitly supplies** Eq(16) second-best first-order gain from infrastructure changes. Therefore the claim "use industrial parks when wage grants unavailable" is ALREADY CONTAINED, NOT an open theorem. A surviving frontier could ONLY be a mathematical result in a precisely specified different local-government policy instrument set and nonseparable distortions which changes a known rank; first examine Fajgelbaum–Gaubert QJE2020 full model, Bilal QJE2023 search frictions, canonical constrained tax theory, and jurisdictional local government spillback before declaring it original. Classification: **EXPLICIT PARENT MODEL LIMIT + ADJACENT THREATS, NOT VERIFIED GLOBAL GAP**.
**S2 — actual residents' incentives vs LOCAL political/bidding competition under restricted policy institutions:** JEL P01 explicitly observes some tax papers maximize revenue rather than resident welfare; P02 JPE firm auction and P12 JPubE career-tournament local procurement make political/firm incentives endogenous; P04 2026 local-vs-national rankings. Therefore "local officials target politically valuable firms" and "local governments bid too much" are ALREADY KNOWN. A meaningful unknown could only be an author-source-explicit incompatibility of welfare equilibrium or mechanism implementability under *specific* local public-funding constraints **not already derived** by P01/P02/P12/P04 and classical political-economy auctions. Need journal-original theorems first. Classification: **HIGH-COLLISION, NO CANDIDATE ADMITTED**.
**S3 — national spatial optimality vs actually decentralized dynamic industry choices:** P08's planner is federal, P05/P04 analyze strategic government investments, P10 handles policy experiments, P17 path-dependence, P06 financing. A new full game would need a theorem that survives these parents AND cannot be written as generic shared-budget free riding or dynamic DP/capability lock-in. Prior failed JRS followups exhibit the risk; do NOT combine all parents in one complex model. Classification: **NOT A SOURCE-CERTIFIED GAP; PRIOR NO-GO STILL APPLIES**.

Alternative exact *published* omissions to register but NOT use as candidate:
