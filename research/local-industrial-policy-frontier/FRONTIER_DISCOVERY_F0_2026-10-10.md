### A. Local information, targeting and experimentation (I01–I04)
I01 — **Local versus national informational advantage:** When central government observes comparable cross-region productivity but local governments have noisy private signals about firms, which allocation of screening/targeting authority maximizes resident welfare? Parent #2,#7,#8,#13; key kill: revelation principle and classic fiscal-federalism local-info result absorb entirely.
I02 — **Strategic truth-telling in joint priority lists:** When local governments request national matched industrial grant funding by recommending firms/sectors, can reporting rules make local rankings truthful without inefficiently changing project selection? Parent #7,#8,#11; kill: textbook mechanism-design private type + intergovernmental grants.
I03 — **Policy experimentation with endogenous pilot selection:** When firms self-select to local pilots and local leaders can exert unreplicable one-shot effort, does nationwide adoption optimally ignore a successful pilot under general welfare objectives? Parent #6,#14; kill: Callander–Harstad plus Wang–Yang already covers main incentive/pilot selection.
I04 — **Market-price informativeness under subsidized trial:** Can a small locality's early industry aid destroy truthful private signals about future viability, creating dynamic learning damage that alters a higher government’s continuation decision? Parent #7,#14; kill: Sockin-Xiong's crowd-out of market information.

### B. Municipal administrative implementation capacity (C01–C04)
C01 — **Endogenous caseworker monitoring bandwidth:** When the ability to verify milestones deteriorates endogenously as the NUMBER of grant contracts rises, how many distinct projects versus subsidy intensity should a local government choose? Parent #2,#12; kill: ordinary fixed managerial capacity/multitasking or screening precision result.
C02 — **Turnover and transferable policy knowledge:** When project quality signals accumulate among mobile/rotating municipal personnel, how does the authority optimally invest in institutionally recorded knowledge vs projects? Parent #2,#7,#12; kill: dynamic organization/knowledge retention classic model, needing exact nearest parent.
C03 — **Evaluation effort under multiple objectives:** When measured jobs, survival, R&D, and resident surplus differ, which verifiable milestone contracts do time-limited caseworkers implement? Parent #2,#7 and general principal-agent; kill: Holmström–Milgrom multitask repeated without sector-specific mechanism.
C04 — **Outsource or internalize project screening:** If specialist support organizations have better technical information but are remunerated per client, when should local authorities delegate screening? ### C. Smart specialization and regional transition (S01–S04)

S03 — **Incumbent cluster lock-in vs entrant support:** When incumbent employer-provided training generates transferable skills but incumbents politically capture support, do innovation subsidies to incumbents reduce future entrants’ productive options? S04 — **Common training infrastructure vs target subsidies:** When a region needs both firms and specific skilled workers to enter, can local skill investment coordinate entry more efficiently than plant subsidies, despite mobile worker exit? ### E. Firm selection, labour-market frictions and subsidy incidence (B01–B04)
