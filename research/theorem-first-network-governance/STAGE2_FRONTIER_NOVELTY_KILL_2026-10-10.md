## 1. The economic question, stripped of industrial-policy labels
Consider two jurisdictions separately choosing vectors of actions, while private firms choose their own supplier-network links after seeing those vectors, followed by equilibrium prices/income and local fiscal incidence. When does the resulting multi-authority Nash select a DIFFERENT network/sector intervention from that chosen by a single constrained welfare maximizer? Is there a substantively new theorem on the efficient-decentralization boundary that fails under an EXOGENOUS network and is not an ordinary tax competition equilibrium or a supplier-switch threshold?

**No proposition has yet been proved for this architecture.** Stage 2 tests which claims are demonstrably old before Stage 3 investment.

## 4. Mathematical whole-game absorption check: a diagnostic accounting theorem, NOT a result worth publishing
Let policy vector be a=(a_1,a_2) and completed downstream market/network equilibrium be E(a), with feasible regional real net utilities V_i(a,E(a)). Require the local financing/tax accounting to satisfy W(a)=V_1(a)+V_2(a) under SAME population and public resource accounting. For any profile in a smooth fixed-network regime:
dW/da_i = dV_i/da_i + dV_j/da_i.
Local interior government FOC dV_i/da_i=0 and constrained national FOC dW/da_i=0 differ by the cross-regional marginal effect. At network edge a*=switch threshold, the SAME additive identity applies to one-sided *finite jumps*:
J_W(a*) = J_i(a*) + J_j(a*).
These are simple arithmetic accounting identities. Neither nonzero dV_j/da_i nor a welfare jump nor switching-policy rank reversal is independent novelty. The policy-induced E(a) dependence is already included in the total derivatives and Poirier's edge jump; regional redistribution changes J_i versus J_j, but that mechanism can be made true/false by **arbitrary local rent shares** without any economic theorem. Examples with central W(0)=10,W(1)=12:
- local allocation of W(0)=(5,5), W(1)=(7,5) -> the policy initiator wants the central improvement.
- local allocation W(0)=(5,5), W(1)=(4,8) -> the initiator OPPOSES the same total welfare improvement.
Both have the same national network welfare change +2. Therefore *claiming a necessarily bad local ranking from an endogenous switch without deriving LOCAL wages/ownership/taxes from market clearing is NOT meaningful*.

This is a conceptual counterexample / diagnostic of insufficiency, NOT an equilibrium theorem nor a full GE. Independent finite arithmetic script accompanies this report.
