## N01a: exact standalone transparent two-region model
Each of two regions i=1,2 has one local downstream final user who obtains fixed final value and requires exactly one intermediate input; each region also has a competitive constant-cost upstream source. Locally funded public cost-reduction g_i∈{0,1} makes the REAL unit labor opportunity cost of upstream output c-g_i, with c≥1. Government spends a real public resource k*g_i, financed by residents without distortion. Input trade incurs real cross-region shipping cost h∈(0,1), zero for domestic. There are competitive supplier prices p_i=c-g_i, zero producer rents; the regional price-taking final buyer j selects supplier a_j in argmin_i {p_i+h 1[i≠j]}, with domestic tie-breaking. Conditional on decisions, final allocations are feasible because local labor endowed at a fixed outside-option wage (numeraire goods), fixed buyer unit demands, supplier capacities sufficient, no double-counted tax expenditure or nominal transfer. Region i's **incremental** quasi-linear real consumption surplus relative to (g1,g2)=(0,0) is
V_i(g)=c-min_{a in {1,2}}{c-g_a+h 1[a≠i]}-k*g_i.
National surplus is S=V1+V2. Buyer-payoff microfoundation counts real transportation plus opportunity cost; competitive zero-profit producers earn no residual regional rents. This is a diagnostic two-region supply-choice REAL-COST game, NOT Poirier's general Cobb–Douglas production-network economy, nor Liu's exogenous market imperfection.

Exhaustive supplier network and local payoffs:
- g=(0,0): both buy domestic, (V1,V2)=(0,0), S=0.
- g=(1,0): BOTH buy supplier region 1, (V1,V2)=(1-k,1-h), S=2-h-k.
- g=(0,1): BOTH buy supplier region 2, (V1,V2)=(1-h,1-k), S=2-h-k.
- g=(1,1): both buy domestic, (V1,V2)=(1-k,1-k), S=2-2k.

Every policy deviation has an exactly solved supplier continuation. For a given other government's g_j:
BR when g_j=0: gain from own investment ΔV_i=1-k.
BR when g_j=1: gain from own investment ΔV_i=h-k.

Thus, away from boundaries k=h,1,2-h:
(i) 0<k<h: UNIQUE pure NE (1,1), planner (1,1), efficiency.
(ii) h<k<1: exactly 2 NE (1,0),(0,1), both planner-optimal, efficiency.
(iii) 1<k<2-h: UNIQUE pure NE (0,0), but planner (1,0) or (0,1), welfare gap 2-h-k>0.
(iv) k>2-h: UNIQUE pure NE (0,0), planner (0,0), efficiency.
At k=h,1,2-h, indifference can create additional equilibrium/optimal profiles; no claim of the above exact NE/optimum correspondence on boundaries.

EXACT example h=1/4, k=6/5: g NE (0,0), planner (1,0)/(0,1), welfare improvement 2-1/4-6/5=11/20. For exogenously FORCED DOMESTIC supplier links, in the same parameter region k>1, no government invests and national planner ALSO never invests. Thus the wedge is FULL-ENDOGENOUS-LINK-dependent. A Python exact-Fraction exhaustive 4-government-action / 4 network-outcome solver tests all unilateral deviations for a grid of admissible rational parameters, as well as the fixed-links control.

**CRITICAL HOSTILE ABSORPTION:** This game can be relabeled as a two-player local-public-good/free-rider adoption game with payoff table:
when one invests, each accesses lower-cost common input after paying a link/transport toll; (0,0) inefficient if costs exceed each own internal return but not the coalition return. This is a textbook fiscal externality from access to another region's cost-saving public input. Endogenous supply links merely produce its spillover schedule. Kinateder–Merlino 2017/2022 already place public-good production and strategic link formation in a richer theoretical class; despite different form and timing, neither an elementary efficiency wedge nor the numerical threshold should be claimed as a new contribution.
