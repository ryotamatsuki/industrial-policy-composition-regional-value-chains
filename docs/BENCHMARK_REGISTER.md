# Benchmark Register

## Decentralized game

- Players: two regional governments.
- Strategy: `x_i in [0,1]`.
- Timing: simultaneous, complete information.
- Equilibrium concept: pure-strategy Nash equilibrium.

## Coordinated benchmark

The planner solves `max_{(x1,x2) in [0,1]^2} W1 + W2` while holding fixed:

- each region's total policy capacity;
- the policy instrument set;
- production technology;
- matching technology;
- information structure.

Canonical label: **coordinated fixed-capacity planner benchmark** (or **coordinated fixed-capacity optimum**).

Explicitly prohibited labels: **first best** and **unrestricted social optimum**.
