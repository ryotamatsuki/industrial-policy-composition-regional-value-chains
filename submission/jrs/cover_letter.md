Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Industrial Policy Composition and Regional Value Chains” for publication as a Research Article in the Journal of Regional Science.

The paper studies a regional-policy problem in which local governments allocate a fixed policy capacity across activities that are complementary across jurisdictions. The baseline model provides an exact characterization of decentralized portfolios and a coordinated fixed-capacity benchmark. Its central result is a positive-measure parameter interval in which both governments uniquely retain the same locally attractive priority even though coordinated reallocation of the same total policy capacity raises modeled aggregate surplus.

The paper is designed as a transparent analytical contribution to regional economics. It does not claim that interjurisdictional spillovers, fixed budgets, or public-policy composition are individually new. Rather, the contribution is the strategic combination of a binding local policy portfolio, vertically complementary regional activities, and incomplete jurisdictional capture, which generates an exact duplication-versus-reallocation threshold result. The manuscript also identifies the scope of that result through a restricted constant-returns extension and an alternative matching technology.

I believe the manuscript fits the Journal of Regional Science because the question concerns regional policy choice and cross-regional economic interaction, while the contribution is primarily analytical and theoretical. The institutional discussion connects the mechanism to regional industrial-policy priority setting, smart specialization, and interregional value-chain initiatives without treating those examples as empirical tests of the model.

The study uses no empirical data. Anonymous reproducibility material supporting the symbolic checks, regression and counterexample tests, deterministic outputs, and documented Lean formalization is supplied as supporting information for peer review. The manuscript also includes a transparent disclosure of material generative-AI assistance consistent with Wiley guidance.

This manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. No external funding supported the work, and I declare no competing interests. No non-AI acknowledgments are included. A public GitHub research repository associated with the project exists and will remain public during review; no separate formal preprint has been identified in the project record.
