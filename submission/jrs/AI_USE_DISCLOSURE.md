## Current Wiley requirement

Current Wiley guidance requires authors to disclose AI Technology used in a submission and to document its purpose, whether it influenced key arguments or conclusions, and how the author reviewed and verified AI-assisted content. AI tools cannot be authors and human authors remain accountable.

Wiley's current disclosure-location guidance distinguishes:

- **Acknowledgments** — AI assistance with manuscript drafting, editing, translation, or formatting;
- **Methods / research-process disclosure** — AI assistance with methodology, data analysis, literature-review processes, or code development;
- **Figure captions** — AI generation or editing of visual content.
